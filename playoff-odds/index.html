METHODOLOGY

How the odds work

Each team's scores through the selected week determine its average score and standard deviation. Every remaining matchup is simulated from those scoring distributions, then the final standings are seeded using the league's ESPN tiebreakers. The same method is used all season, so early-season results may be extreme and naturally stabilize as more scores are recorded.
