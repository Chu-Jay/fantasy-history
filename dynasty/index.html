TERMS KEY

Prestige

Overall fantasy success, weighting regular season, postseason, scoring, consistency, and longevity-related results.

Consistency

How regularly a manager performs as one of the league's stronger weekly scorers. The raw score is shown separately so it does not determine the overall grade by itself.

Dynasty Grade

An overall tier based on Prestige relative to the managers currently included in the comparison. It is not a consistency-only grade.

Luck

Total win percentage minus average likelihood to win, showing the difference between results and expected weekly success.
